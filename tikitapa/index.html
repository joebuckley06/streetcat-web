Tap Soccer 2026

Coming to the App Store

A tactical tap-based soccer game built around the 2026 World Cup window. Quick matches that reward thinking — not thumb speed.

The Pitch

Every match unfolds in discrete ticks. You tap to move, pass, or shoot; the opponent does the same; the engine resolves both decisions simultaneously. The faster you play (Premier speed unlocked at higher difficulties), the less time you have to read the field. Easy on the fingers, harder than it looks.

Modes

VS Computer

Four AI difficulties · 8v8 or 11v11

Quick singles with any of 48 national teams. Pick your tactic, scout the opponent, kick off.

Tournament

Group stage → knockouts → final

A full World Cup bracket against AI. Difficulty ratchets each round; one tournament win unlocks that country's alternate kit.

Multiplayer

Live head-to-head over the internet

Play friends with a room code, or jump into matchmaking against a stranger. Premier-speed cadence by default.
