Privacy Policy

Tiki-Tapa: Tap Soccer 2026

Effective date: 2026-06-02 · Publisher: StreetCat Creative LLC · Contact: joe@streetcatcreative.org

This Privacy Policy describes how the Tiki-Tapa: Tap Soccer 2026 mobile application (the "App") collects, uses, and shares information when you play. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

We try to collect as little as we can — there's no email/password signup, no advertising profile we build, and no personal data sold to third parties. This document explains exactly what we do collect, why, and your choices.

1. What we collect

1.1 Account identifiers (required to play)

  • Anonymous user ID issued by Firebase Authentication the first time you launch. This is a random string with no tie to your real identity unless you sign in with Apple (see below).
  • Sign in with Apple identifier, name, and email (optional, only if you link your account). When you link, Apple sends us a pseudonymous user identifier, your name (only on first sign-in), and your email address — either your real Apple ID email or, if you chose to hide it, a unique Apple-managed private-relay address ending in @privaterelay.appleid.com. These are stored by Firebase Authentication as part of your account record so we can recover your profile across reinstalls. We do not read your name or email anywhere in the App; you appear to other players using only the in-App username and display name you set yourself.

1.2 Profile information (you set this yourself)

  • Username (lowercase handle), display name, favorite team. You can change or clear these at any time in the Account screen.
  • Optional details: full name, contact email, and age, if you choose to enter them on the profile screen. All three are optional — you can skip them, and you can clear them later from the Account screen. We use them only to personalise your profile and to understand our audience in aggregate; we do not email you, and we never share them with advertisers.

1.3 Gameplay data

  • Match results: scores, opponents, formations, timestamps, country picks, tournament progress.
  • Multiplayer ephemeral data: room code, real-time positions and actions during an active head-to-head match. This is cleared after the match ends.
  • Friends: a list of other users you've added.

1.4 Device identifier for advertising (only with your consent)

  • If you tap Allow on Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, we share Apple's IDFA (a per-device advertising ID, resettable from iOS Settings) with Google AdMob so ads shown to you are more relevant.
  • If you tap Ask App Not to Track, we don't share the IDFA. You still see ads — they're just less targeted.

1.5 In-app purchases

  • Purchases (Remove Ads, Pro Pass, group kit bundles, and the All Kits bundle) are processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. We see a receipt that tells us which product you bought; we never see your credit card or Apple Pay details.

1.6 Crash and diagnostic data

  • We do not currently run a third-party crash reporter. If you opt into iOS-level analytics sharing in Settings, Apple sends us anonymized crash reports through Xcode Organizer; we use these only to fix bugs.

1.7 Push notification token

  • If you grant notification permission, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token so we can deliver multiplayer match-invite pushes through Apple's Push Notification Service. The token is generated by Apple / Firebase, rotates periodically, and is not linked to your real identity. Turn off notifications for Tiki-Tapa in iOS Settings to stop us from receiving it.

1.8 Analytics and measurement

  • Usage events. We record in-app actions such as screens viewed, matches started and finished, levels reached, badges unlocked, shop visits, and purchase attempts. We use these to understand which parts of the game people enjoy and where they get stuck.
  • Analytics identifier. When you are signed in, we associate your analytics activity with your account ID so we can understand how features are used across sessions and devices. This identifier is internal to our analytics and is not shared with advertisers.
  • Advertising performance data. When an ad is shown, we record the estimated revenue of that impression, the ad format, and which placement it appeared in. This tells us which parts of the App sustain the free version. It is not tied to the content of any ad you saw.
  • Aggregate characteristics. We store coarse groupings — such as an age range like "25-34" rather than your exact age, an experience level range, and whether you have made a purchase — so we can understand our audience as groups rather than individuals. Your age range is never shared with advertising networks.

We do not collect: phone number, precise location, address book contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, or financial details. Name and email from Sign in with Apple are covered above (§1.1); they are stored only by Firebase Authentication and not read or used elsewhere in the App.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited data with the following service providers, and only to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config). Stores your profile, friends list, match history, and multiplayer room state; records the usage events described in §1.8; delivers push notifications; and lets us adjust in-app settings without shipping an update. Google's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob (advertising). Receives the IDFA only if you allowed tracking; otherwise receives no identifier. Used to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob & data.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent management for GDPR users). Records your ad-consent choices.
  • Meta (Facebook) (app-install measurement). Receives a limited set of milestone events — for example that a registration, a first completed match, a level-up, or a purchase occurred, and the purchase amount — so we can tell which advertising campaigns bring in players who enjoy the game. If you did not allow tracking, Meta receives these only in aggregated, non-identifying form via Apple's SKAdNetwork. We never send Meta your age, your profile details, or your match history. Meta's privacy policy.
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store, StoreKit, App Tracking Transparency). Processes authentication and purchases; you interact with Apple directly. Apple's privacy policy.

Other multiplayer users see your username, display name, country pick, and live match actions while you are in a match together. They never see your account identifiers.

We may disclose data if compelled by law (subpoena, court order, etc.), but we'll push back where appropriate.

4. How long we keep it

  • Active profile + match history: kept as long as your account exists.
  • Multiplayer room state: deleted automatically minutes after the match ends.
  • IAP receipts: kept for the life of your account so we can restore purchases.
  • Account deletion: if you delete your account from the Account screen, your profile, friends list, and match history are permanently removed within 30 days. Anonymous identifiers tied only to non-personal aggregates may persist in backups for a short additional period.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop ad tracking: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off for Tiki-Tapa.
  • Change or clear profile data: Account screen inside the App.
  • Restore purchases: Shop → Restore Purchases.
  • Delete your account: Account screen → Delete Account. This is permanent.
  • Access or export your data: email us at the address above; we'll respond within 30 days.
  • EU/UK users have the rights under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection); California users have rights under CCPA (know, delete, opt out of sale — we do not sell). Exercise any of these by emailing us.

6. Children's privacy

Tiki-Tapa is intended for users 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS / TLS) for all data in transit and rely on Google Firebase's at-rest encryption for stored data. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

8. International transfers

Your data is processed on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be located in the United States or other regions. By using the App you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, notify you in-app on next launch.
