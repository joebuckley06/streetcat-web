Privacy Policy

Monday Minute

Effective date: 2026-11-10 · Publisher: StreetCat Creative LLC · Contact: contact@streetcatcreative.org

Monday Minute (the "App") lets small groups of friends share a short voice message each week. This policy explains what the App collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Monday Minute is in a private beta.

1. What we collect

1.1 Your account

  • Name you choose for your profile.
  • Email address, if you sign in with email, or the one Apple or Google shares when you sign in with them (with Apple you can choose to hide your email).
  • A sign-in identifier from Firebase Authentication, and from Apple or Google if you use them. We never see your password.

1.2 Your profile and groups

  • An optional profile photo and "about" line, and the date you joined.
  • A username you choose, so friends can find you.
  • Groups you create or join: their names, pictures, weekly prompt and settings, members, and invite codes.

1.3 Friends and invitations

  • Friend requests and friendships: who you asked, who asked you, and who you are friends with.
  • Group invitations you send or receive.
  • A searchable profile (your name, username, photo and about line) so other people in the App can find you by username or name. It does not include your email address.

1.4 Your messages

  • Voice messages you send, their length, and a small loudness outline (a "waveform") used to draw the player.
  • Who has listened to each message.

1.5 Other

  • If you turn on notifications, a push token for your phone, so we can notify you about friend requests, group invitations and group activity. You can turn notifications off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Your time zone, to schedule your groups' weeks.
  • Standard technical data, such as IP addresses, that Google processes to run and secure the service.
  • If you test through Apple's TestFlight or Google Play, crash reports and any feedback you choose to send reach us through them.

The App uses the microphone only while you record, and the camera or photo library only when you pick a photo. Drafts and unsent recordings stay on your phone until you send or delete them. We don't collect your location or contacts, and the App has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

2. Why we collect it

Only to run the App: to sign you in, show your groups, deliver your messages to your group, and keep the service working and secure.

3. Who can see it

  • Members of your groups see your name, photo, about line, the messages you send to that group, and whether you've heard theirs.
  • Other people in the App can find your name, username, photo and about line by searching, and can send you a friend request. They can't see your messages or groups unless you share a group.
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) stores and processes the data in the United States. Apple and Google take part in sign-in if you use them, and Apple's and Google's notification services deliver push notifications to your phone.

We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for ads.

4. How long we keep it

Messages stay in the group's weekly archive until their author deletes them, the group is deleted, or the author deletes their account. A group is deleted when its last member leaves. Your account details stay until you delete your account.

5. Delete your account

In the App, open Me and tap Delete account. This deletes your messages, replies and voice recordings from every group, removes you from all your groups (if you were a group's admin, another member takes over, and a group left with no members is deleted), and deletes your profile, username, searchable profile, profile photo, friend requests, friendships, group invitations, phone push tokens and sign-in account. Group pictures you set stay with the group. It can't be undone.

If you can't use the App, email contact@streetcatcreative.org from the address on your account, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The App isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access rules let only a group's members read its messages and group details. Other signed-in people can read only your name, username, photo and about line, never your email address.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and tell testers in the App's release notes.
