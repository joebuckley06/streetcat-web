Weekly voice notes with your friends · Beta

A friend invited you to their group. Install the beta, then join with the code from their message.

Your phone

iPhone

Get the iPhone beta
  1. If you don't have it yet, install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the Monday Minute beta on your iPhone, tap Accept, then Install.

Your phone

Android

Get the Android beta
  1. First, send your friend the Gmail address you use for Google Play, so they can add you to the beta. The link won't work until they have.
  2. On your phone, signed in with that Google account, open the Monday Minute beta, tap Become a tester, then install it from Google Play.
  3. Google Play may show the name "com.joebuckley.mondayminute (unreviewed)" while the beta is new. That's us. Once installed it's called Monday Minute.

Then

Open Monday Minute, sign in, tap Join with a code and enter the code from your invite.
